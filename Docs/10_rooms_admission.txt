MEDCLEAR GENERAL HOSPITAL — ROOMS AND ADMISSION

ADMISSION DESK

Location:
Ground Floor near the main reception.

GENERAL WARD

Shared accommodation is available subject to hospital capacity.

PRIVATE ROOM

Private rooms may be available depending on hospital capacity.

ROOM AVAILABILITY

The AI assistant should not claim that a specific room is available unless it has access to the live hospital admission system.

ADMISSION

Admission may require:
- Patient registration
- Doctor's admission recommendation
- Required documentation
- Completion of admission procedures

DISCHARGE

Discharge depends on:
- Treating doctor's instructions
- Completion of required medical procedures
- Completion of billing procedures
- Hospital discharge process

IMPORTANT

The AI assistant should not promise admission or room availability without checking the appropriate hospital system.
