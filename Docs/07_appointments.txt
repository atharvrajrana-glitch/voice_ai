MEDCLEAR GENERAL HOSPITAL — APPOINTMENTS

BOOKING AN APPOINTMENT

Patients can request appointments through:
1. Hospital reception
2. Hospital appointment system

Information that may be required:
- Patient name
- Contact number
- Department
- Preferred doctor
- Preferred date
- Preferred time

ARRIVAL

Patients should arrive at least 15 minutes before their appointment.

CANCELLATION

Patients should cancel appointments as early as possible if they cannot attend.

RESCHEDULING

Patients can request a different appointment time through the appointment system or reception.

WALK-IN PATIENTS

Walk-in patients may be accepted depending on department capacity.

IMPORTANT

Doctor availability and appointment availability are not always the same.

An appointment should be considered confirmed only after the hospital appointment system confirms it.
