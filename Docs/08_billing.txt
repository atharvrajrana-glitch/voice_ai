Hours:

PAYMENT METHODS

The hospital accepts:
- Cash
- Debit cards
- Credit cards
- Supported digital payment methods

BILLING QUESTIONS

Patients can contact the billing desk for questions about:
- Hospital bills
- Payment status
- Receipts
- Refund requests

REFUNDS

Refund requests are handled according to hospital policy.

IMPORTANT

The AI assistant should not invent treatment costs or billing amounts.

If a specific price is not present in the hospital knowledge base, the assistant should tell the patient that the price information is unavailable and direct them to the billing department.
