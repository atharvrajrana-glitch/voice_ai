MEDCLEAR GENERAL HOSPITAL — INSURANCE

INSURANCE DESK

Location:
Ground Floor near the billing department.

INSURANCE VERIFICATION

Patients should confirm whether their insurance plan covers a planned service before receiving that service.

DOCUMENTS

Patients may be asked to provide:
- Insurance card
- Identification
- Policy information
- Relevant hospital documents

CASHLESS SERVICES

Availability of cashless services depends on the patient's insurance provider and hospital agreements.

CLAIM QUESTIONS

Insurance claim questions should be directed to the insurance desk.

IMPORTANT

The AI assistant must not guarantee insurance approval.

If insurance information is unavailable, the assistant should recommend contacting the insurance desk.
