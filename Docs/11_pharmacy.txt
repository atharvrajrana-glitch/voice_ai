SERVICES

The pharmacy provides medicines and other approved hospital pharmacy products according to hospital procedures.

PRESCRIPTIONS

Patients should provide a valid prescription when required.

MEDICINE AVAILABILITY

The AI assistant should not claim that a specific medicine is available unless it can check the live pharmacy inventory.

MEDICINE ADVICE

The AI assistant should not independently prescribe medicines or change a patient's prescribed dosage.

For medication-related questions, patients should be directed to an appropriate doctor or pharmacist.
